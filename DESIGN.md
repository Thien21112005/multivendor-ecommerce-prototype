# Design System: Trao

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Glass Ledger"**

A light field carries the top of the page: sky at the top melting into a warm paper ground, with one marigold light in the corner. Frosted white glass floats on it for the two things that orient the buyer: the navigation bar, and the money rail that shows where their money is right now. Products and prices never sit on glass; they sit on solid white sheets on paper, so the eye moves from a luminous layer to a calm, legible one. Navy is the ink, royal blue is for actions, and marigold marks money and the one thing to look at.

The 2026-11 revision replaced the deep blue field, the blue flash band and the sky-to-blue gradients after the owner found the all-blue page tiring and too close to Tiki. Blue now covers buttons, links and the footer only.

The structure comes from a ledger: a status line for the money, a seal for each verified shop, the next step shown first. The material of that ledger is translated into glass. On the home page, the promise is a headline whose money half sits on a marigold highlighter stroke, proved at once by a working rail laid over a shelf of priced product photographs. On buying screens the same promise returns as a small hold note, and the density stays that of a working marketplace, with 14px body text.

The name "Trao" and the droplet logo (solid action blue with a marigold check) are provisional. The owner has not chosen a final name, so nothing here depends on the word or on the logo's exact drawing. The rename tool does not touch Markdown files, so a rename means updating this file by hand.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Light sky-to-paper field with a marigold light behind the header and hero; no full-width blue bands except the footer.
- Blurred glass on exactly two components: the navigation bar and the money rail.
- Solid white sheets on a warm paper ground for everything that is read, compared or bought.
- Ink prices, marigold for money and attention, red kept for discounts, errors and destructive actions.
- One signature motion: a marigold coin moving along the money rail, one pass to "Shop nhận tiền", then still until clicked, and absent under reduced motion.
- Fine-line seals generated from each shop's id, so no two shops share a mark.

## Colors

Navy ink and royal action blue on warm paper, one marigold accent for money and attention, sky as light rather than as a fill, and status hues held to their own meanings.

### Primary
- **Action Blue** (#1D4ED8): Primary buttons, search submit, links, seal linework, checked controls and focus. Never a page band or a section background. Its hover (#1A43BD) and pressed (#16379B) states are the only darker steps used for action.
- **Midnight Blue** (#0B1F4B): Headlines and text on the field and on glass, the wordmark, the chat launcher, the countdown chip, the footer surface and hold-note text.
- **Marigold** (#FFC02E, hover #F2A900): The money accent. The rail coin, the highlighter under the hero's money words, voucher save buttons, chat and nav count badges, countdown digits on midnight, the active icon in Vendor and Admin navigation, and the current period in charts. Always under midnight text (10:1); never marigold text or icons on white. **Marigold Ink** (#6E4E00) is the text form on white; **Marigold Wash** (#FFF7E0) with **Marigold Edge** (#FFE9AE) is the flash sheet, the points card and waiting work in dashboards.
- **Voucher Ink** (#1B2438): The shop voucher stub, separating it from the action-blue platform stub without using red.
- **Action Tint** (#EEF3FF): Hover on secondary buttons and shop rows, unread notifications, selected list items. **Action Tint Strong** (#DCE6FE) is the pressed step.
- **Secondary Border** (#B9CDFB): The quiet outline of secondary buttons on the storefront.

### Secondary
- **Field Sky** (#D2EEFC) to **Sky Wash** (#EAF7FE) to **Page Paper**: The three stops of the light field, top to bottom. A sky glow (#74C8F3 at 42 percent) sits top left and a marigold light (34 percent) top right, so the glass has colour to bend.
- **Sky Wash** (#EAF7FE): Also the hold-note background, always with midnight text.
- **Footer Text** (#C9D6EE) and **Footer Legal** (#AFC0E0): Text on the midnight footer.
- **Glass White** (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)): The glass tint, used only with the blur and rim described under Elevation & Depth.

### Tertiary
- **Discount Red** (#C8103F): Percent-off badges and the Flash Sale badge. **Discount Red Deep** (#A50D34), **Discount Wash** (#FFEDF1) and **Sold Bar** (#FFD3DD) make up the sold-progress bar.

- **Info Cyan** (#0B7FAB, deep #08688D, wash #E5F5FB): The information role and the "paid" order status. It leans cyan so that it never reads as the brand.
- **Freeship Mint** (#0B7F4C, deep #09693F, wash #E6F8F0): Free shipping, freeship vouchers, savings lines and delivered orders.

- **Rating Gold** (#F2A900): Star icons, always beside a numeric rating. It is the marigold family, so ratings, points and money read as one value colour.
- **Shipped Violet** (#6C2BD9, wash #F1ECFE): The shipped order status only.

### Neutral
- **Sheet White** (#FFFFFF): Reading surfaces, cards, popovers, the search field.
- **Page Paper** (#F7F5F0, `--paper-50`): The page behind the sheets in all three apps. **Page Mist** (#F3F6FA) stays for sunken wells inside sheets. **Sunken Gray** (#E9EDF3) is the image placeholder and the subtle divider.
- **Border Default** (#DCE2EB) and **Border Strong** (#C4CDD9): Field outlines, row dividers, outline buttons.
- **Ink Text** (#121824): Headings on sheets and all prices.
- **Body Text** (#242C39), **Rail Text** (#36404F), **Muted Text** (#4A5568), **Subtle Text** (#647084), **Disabled Text** (#98A3B3): The reading ramp, from body copy to disabled controls. Struck-through prior prices use Subtle Text.
- **Error Tint Border** (#F3B0A8, `--red-200`) and **Deep Gold** (#6E4E00, `--gold-700`): the border of error callouts, and gold text that must pass contrast on white.
- **Ink Chrome** (#111827), **Ink Chrome Deep** (#0A0F1C), **Ink Chrome Raised** (#1B2438): Dark chrome for Vendor and Admin, plus toasts in those apps. The storefront does not use them for chrome.

### Named Rules
**The Ink Price Rule.** Every price is Ink Text with tabular figures. A price in blue reads as a link; a price in red reads as a shouting sale.

**The Rationed Red Rule.** Red means a discount, an error or a destructive action, and nothing else. It does not mark urgency, favourites, required fields or decoration. The flash sheet is marigold wash; only its percent badges and sold bars are red.

**The Midnight Count Rule.** Count badges in the header are Midnight Blue with white figures on glass. On a midnight surface (the chat launcher, the dark nav of Vendor and Admin) the count flips to Marigold with midnight figures. A count is information, not an alarm.

**The Blue Is For Doing Rule.** Royal blue marks what can be pressed: buttons, links, focus. It never fills a page band, a section or a hero. Large dark areas are Midnight and limited to the footer, the chat launcher and the auth panel.

**The One Marigold Rule.** Marigold is a highlight, never a surface larger than a button, except as the pale wash. Each view has a handful of marigold marks, and each one is about money or the one thing to look at.

**The Sky Is Light Rule.** Sky is never a solid fill behind white text. It forms the top of the light field and may tint a surface carrying midnight text.

**The Cyan Is Not Brand Rule.** The `--blue-*` tokens are the information role. Brand work uses `--brand-*`; a button, general link or selected state is never drawn from `--blue-*`. An inline link inside an information note may stay in the information colour.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Be Vietnam Pro (with system-ui fallback)
**Body Font:** Be Vietnam Pro (with system-ui fallback)
**Label/Mono Font:** IBM Plex Mono, for order codes, account numbers and transfer references

**Character:** One Vietnamese-first geometric sans does everything, from a very heavy, tightly tracked headline to dense 12px metadata. The range between the two is the whole typographic voice, and the mono appears only where a value must be copied or checked character by character.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp(36px, 5.2vw, 72px), 1.14, -0.03em): The hero promise on home only, in Midnight with the money half on a marigold highlighter.
- **Headline** (800, clamp(24px, 2.6vw, 34px), 1.15): Section headings on the page.
- **Title** (600, 22px, 1.3) and **Title Small** (600, 18px, 1.3): Product names on the detail page and panel headings.
- **Lede** (400, clamp(16px, 1.4vw, 19px), 1.5): The sentence under the hero in Rail Text, held to 62ch.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 1.5) and **Body Small** (400, 13px, 1.5): Default copy, card titles clamped to two lines, rail notes and hold notes.
- **Label** (600, 13px, 1.3) and **Caption** (400, 12px, 1.3): Field labels, nav text, shop and rating metadata.
- **Price Card** (800, 17px, 1.2) and **Price Detail** (700, 34px, 1): Prices at card and product-page scale, tabular.

### Named Rules
**The Stacked Diacritic Rule.** Headlines that can wrap keep a line height of at least 1.14, so stacked Vietnamese marks such as those in "Nhận" and "đến" never touch the line above. Check every new heading against real Vietnamese copy.

**The Tabular Money Rule.** Money, counts and countdowns use tabular figures so that columns and ticking clocks do not jitter.

## Layout

The page is a single centred column, 1280px wide with a 24px gutter (12px on phones). Sections sit 56px apart (36px on phones). Density is deliberately high below the hero: products run five across, then three at 1100px and two at 768px, with gaps of 18px by 14px.

The light field is an absolutely positioned layer behind the header. On inner pages it is 148px tall (178px on phones), just enough to carry the bar and the category row before the content sheets begin. On home it grows and ends partway down the product shelf, at 60 percent of the shelf and rail stage, wherever the headline happened to wrap; it fades into the paper, so there is no edge.

The home first viewport layers in order: floating bar, category row, headline, lede, a six-across shelf of clickable photographs (200px to 276px tall) each carrying its price on a small glass tag, then the rail overlapping the bottom of the shelf by 60px. Below the hero come the Admin banner carousel, picks, the middle banner, the flash sheet with vouchers, and verified shops. On phones the shelf becomes a horizontal scroller of 44vw items, the search field drops to its own row in the bar, and the rail hides its secondary hint.

The bar is sticky with a 12px top offset (8px on phones). The category row stays in the page flow and scrolls sideways when it overflows. Breakpoints are 1100px, 900px and 768px, and the system is checked from 320px to 1440px, including phone landscape. Spacing is built on the 4px grid. The hit-target floor is 44px on phones.

On phones the task comes first, so screens are rethought rather than shrunk:
- **The decision stays in thumb reach.** Product and checkout keep a fixed bottom bar; the cart keeps a sticky total and button (from 900px down), while its tools stay in the page.
- **Task screens drop the category row** so the content starts sooner: product, cart, checkout, receipt, tracking and account.
- **Filters open as a full sheet** from a button in the sort bar, closing on "Xem N sản phẩm", so results lead.
- **Long lists fold.** Checkout shows the first line of each shop with "Xem thêm", the footer folds into groups, and the account menu and order tabs scroll sideways with the current item in view.
- **Chat gets out of the way.** The bubble hides where a bottom bar exists, and chat still opens from "Chat ngay".

Accessibility and language rules, held at zero WCAG 2.1 AA violations (axe) on the main screens:
- **Controls and labels.** A `Checkbox`, `Radio` or `Switch` without its own text renders without a `<label>` and takes an `aria-label`, so a surrounding label still names it.
- **Keyboard.** A clickable `span` or `div` gets `role="button"`, `tabIndex={0}` and `onKeyDown={window.LZKey}`. Focus shows a 2px brand outline. The storefront starts with a skip link, and search suggestions follow the combobox pattern with arrow keys.
- **Accessible names.** Icon buttons with a count include the count in their name.
- **Words.** Status codes never reach people: `StatusBadge` translates them, and buyers read "Trao giữ tiền", never "escrow". Error codes, when kept, are secondary to a plain sentence.
- **Demo controls.** They live in a dashed `DemoBox` labelled "Mô phỏng cho buổi demo", closed by default and away from real actions, or carry "Mô phỏng" in their label.
- **Releasing money is confirmed.** Confirming receipt states that Trao will pay the shop.

Layering: sticky bar 100, cart total 90, bottom bars 800, chat 850, modals 900, filter sheet 4100. Never set a z-index on `.tr-main`, and never use `overflow: hidden` on `html` or `body`: the first traps overlays under the header, the second silently disables every sticky element.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is a hybrid with a strict split. Glass floats with a blur, a light rim and a navy shadow; reading sheets stay opaque and barely lifted. Product cards and home lists are flat on Page Mist, and working panels on inner screens take at most the Small shadow. Popovers lift off with the largest shadow and stay solid white.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Glass** (`inset 0 1px 0 #fff, inset 0 -10px 18px -14px rgba(11,31,75,.14), 0 0 0 1px rgba(11,31,75,.06), 0 16px 36px -14px rgba(11,31,75,.28)`): Glass only, together with `backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(160%)` (14px blur on phones), a 1px white rim at 90 percent and a white tint at 80 percent (84 percent on the home rail, so photographs behind it stay a soft wash rather than a smear). The navy hairline keeps the glass visible on the light field.
- **Popover** (`0 24px 48px rgba(11,31,75,.2)`): Search suggestions, mini cart, notifications, account menu, modals.
- **Small** (`0 1px 3px rgba(11,31,75,.08), 0 1px 2px rgba(11,31,75,.04)`): Working panels on inner screens, and small floating controls such as the favourite button and the voucher save tag.

### Named Rules
**The Glass Is For Finding Your Way Rule.** Blurred glass belongs to the navigation bar and the money rail, nothing else. Products, prices, forms, popovers and modals are solid. Plain translucent white without blur, such as category chips on the field, is not glass and is allowed there. The price tags on shelf photographs and the carousel arrows use a small 8px blur; they are labels, not reading surfaces.

**The Quiet Sheet Rule.** A reading sheet never goes beyond the Small shadow and never gets a blur or a tint. Product cards take no shadow at all.

**The Opaque Fallback Rule.** Under `prefers-reduced-transparency`, glass becomes solid white with no blur. Everything on glass must already be legible on white.

## Shapes

The form language is soft and generous, and radius tells you the layer. The floating bar is 26px (22px on phones) and the rail 28px; product cards, home lists and shelf photographs 20px; working panels, vouchers and the search field 16px; fields and hold notes 10px. Storefront buttons and chips are full pills, and the search submit sits as a 12px block inside its 16px field. Vouchers are white tickets with a coloured stub and a dashed perforation line. The two curved marks are the marigold coin on the rail and the rosette seal, both drawn as SVG.

## Components

### Buttons
Confident pills that keep the marketplace's compact height.
- **Shape:** Full pill (999px) on the storefront, 44px minimum height, 52px for large.
- **Primary:** Action Blue with white text, the main action on a screen.
- **Hover / Focus:** Steps to the hover blue, then the pressed blue with a 1px downward nudge. Focus shows a 3px ring of action blue at 40 percent. Transitions run 120ms with the out curve.
- **Secondary:** White with Action Blue text and the Secondary Border, tinting on hover. **Outline** is white with Body Text and Border Strong, turning blue on hover. **Ghost** is transparent, for dismissals. **Danger** is Error Red, for destructive confirmation only.

### Chips
- **Style:** Category chips on the light field are 36px pills: 62 percent white with a 10 percent midnight border, Midnight medium-weight text and an icon.
- **State:** Hover turns the fill solid white. Focus uses a double ring, 3px white then 2px action blue, so it reads on any part of the field.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 20px on product cards and home lists, 16px on working panels.
- **Background:** Sheet White on Page Paper.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Per the Quiet Sheet Rule. A product card responds by scaling its image to 1.04 over 280ms rather than lifting.
- **Border:** None on product cards. Working panels may carry a 1px Sunken Gray hairline.
- **Internal Padding:** 10px 12px 12px in the product card body (8px horizontally on phones), about 16px to 22px in working panels.
- **Product card contents:** Square image, two-line title, ink price with struck prior price, seal with shop name and rating, optional mint free-shipping line. A red percent badge sits bottom right on the image, a midnight status flag bottom left, a round 36px favourite button top right.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** White, 1.5px Border Default, 10px radius, 44px minimum height, Label above and Caption hint below.
- **Focus:** Border turns Action Blue with the 3px focus ring.
- **Error / Disabled:** Error border and Error Red Deep caption; disabled fields sit on Page Mist with Disabled Text.

### Navigation
A floating glass bar holds the logo, search, actions and account, and stays in view on scroll. Text and icons are Midnight Blue, with 8 percent midnight on hover. The search field is a solid white well in the glass with an inset hairline, turning to a 2px inset action blue on focus, and its suggestions open on a solid white popover. Count badges follow the Midnight Count Rule. Below 1100px the seller link, language and help actions fold away; on phones the search takes its own row and the account shows only its avatar.

### Money Rail (signature)
The buyer's money as a four-stop line: buyer pays, the platform holds, buyer receives, shop gets paid. A marigold coin with a midnight lock rides the line and squashes as it lands. It moves every 3.6 seconds for one pass to "Shop nhận tiền" and then holds still; a click takes over at any time. The line fills in Action Blue Pressed (#16379B), the current stop is bold Midnight, finished stops have the same blue rings, and one sentence below changes with the stop and is announced politely to screen readers. On home it is interactive glass over the shelf, with its sample amount labelled as an example. On the payment receipt it becomes a fixed, solid white rail showing the buyer's real total at "held"; the solid version carries no blur, rim or shadow.

### Hold Note
The same promise placed beside a buy decision: shield icon in Action Blue, Midnight text on Sky Wash, 10px radius. It appears on the product page and at checkout, naming the real shop or the real amount. The cart total carries the same promise as one line with a shield icon, because space there is tight.

### Money Chip
The state of one order's money, on every order card in the buyer's order list: a small pill with a shield icon. "Trao đang giữ tiền" (Sky Wash, Action Blue) while the platform holds it, "Đã chuyển cho shop" once released, "Đã hoàn tiền" after a refund. It sits beside the payment-method chip and the order status, so the buyer never has to open an order to know where the money is.

### Promise Strip
Three short cards under the shop row on the product page, headed "Khi mua ở Trao": the platform holds the money until delivery, 7 days to request a return, and the shop's verification with its real start year and rating. Each is an Action Blue line icon, a bold sentence-case title and one supporting line. No uppercase labels, no badges.

### Payment Gateway (mock)
Checkout's VNPAY step opens a solid white dialog titled "(mô phỏng)": the receiver is named as Trao's holding account, the amount is shown large in ink, and a small Money Rail sits at "Bạn thanh toán". On pay, the coin travels to "Trao giữ tiền" and the sentence below changes before the receipt opens. A safe "Quay lại đơn hàng" action takes first focus, and one line states that no real transaction takes place. It never imitates VNPAY's own branding.

### Dialogs
Every modal uses one dialog: `role="dialog"` with a labelled title, focus moves in on open and is trapped inside, Esc closes it, focus returns to the control that opened it, and the close button is a 44px target. Dialogs that confirm money (receipt confirmation, payout approval) restate the amount and the consequence in the body, not only in the button.

### Shop Seal
A verified shop's mark: a fine-line rosette generated from the shop id, three curves with a filled centre and initial at 28px and above, one heavier curve below that. It sits beside the shop name on product cards, cart and checkout groups, the verified shop list, the product page and the shop page.

### Vouchers
White 16px-radius tickets on a 1px Border Default edge. The amount sits on an 80px stub (Action Blue for platform, Voucher Ink for shop, Freeship Mint for shipping), a dashed hairline separates it from the kind and condition, and the save action is inside the ticket: a marigold "Lưu" pill, which becomes a green check on a green wash once saved (its name says "Đã lưu"). Tickets run in an auto-fill grid of at least 250px, one column on phones.

### Flash Band
A marigold-wash sheet (28px radius, marigold edge) inside the page column, with an ink headline, a midnight countdown chip whose digits are marigold, white product sheets and the voucher tickets under "Lưu voucher, dùng khi thanh toán". The countdown runs to midnight today, so a reload never restarts it. Urgency comes from the clock and the sold bars, not from a red or blue surface.

### Admin Banners
Every position in Admin → Banner has a place on the storefront, read from one store (`window.LZ.banners`, seeded so a fresh browser shows them): the home top carousel under the rail (every live banner in Admin order, 6 seconds each, paused on hover or focus, arrows and marigold dots), the middle banner before Flash Sale, the flash banner inside the flash sheet, the category-top carousel on its category page, and the popup once per session. Banners are 4:1 on desktop and 2:1 on phones, with the Admin mobile image used under 768px. Admin names where each position appears and links to it with "Xem".

### Vendor and Admin
Kênh người bán and the Admin Console share this palette, type family and name, but not this world. They keep their own dark ink sidebars (Ink Chrome for Vendor, Ink Chrome Deep for Admin), 10px buttons and solid white sheets. Glass, the blue field and pill buttons are scoped to the storefront.

Their overview screens are built from `genie/dash.jsx` and `genie/dash.css`:
- **Work queue first.** A dashboard opens with what needs doing: a count, a plain label and a hint, each linking to the screen that resolves it. Waiting work sits on Marigold Wash; an empty queue turns to a quiet outline.
- **One stat tile.** Label, figure, then a comparison that names its period. No icon chips, no coloured borders. A tile is a button only when it leads somewhere.
- **One period control.** Every figure it governs moves together. Balances that do not depend on the period say so.
- **Honest charts, gray first.** Past periods are gray; only the current period is coloured (the last bar in Action Blue Pressed, the last line point a marigold dot on a navy ink line). A zero baseline and a labelled axis, the peak labelled once, a tooltip on hover and keyboard, and a table view. Only closed periods appear, never future months.
- **Parts of a whole are one bar.** Money held by the platform and a shop's money are a single stacked bar with a legend that carries the amounts and shares; each legend row links to its screen. Status tones: held amber, released green, pending payout info cyan, waiting gray, refunds red.
- **Orders as a pipeline.** Orders still moving read left to right in processing order, each in its status colour, with how finished orders ended (completed, cancelled, returned, with shares) on one line below.
- **Selected navigation** is a 10 percent white fill with a marigold icon, not a blue block.
- **One source for every number.** Counts come from the lists they summarise and totals are derived, so a dashboard, its nav badge and its detail screen cannot disagree. TechZone's revenue is the same figure in both apps.
- **Finance follows permission.** Money and per-shop GMV need `VIEW_FINANCE`, and each role sees only the queues it can act on.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep every reading surface solid white, with glass reserved for the navigation bar and the money rail.
- **Do** set every price in Ink Text with tabular figures, at every scale including small popovers.
- **Do** use Midnight Blue for count badges on glass, and Marigold under midnight figures on midnight surfaces.
- **Do** repeat the money promise beside each buying decision with the Hold Note, and show the solid rail with real values once money has moved.
- **Do** show a Shop Seal beside the shop name in buying contexts.
- **Do** stop automatic motion on first interaction and remove it entirely under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Do** give every new control a 44px minimum hit target, including icon buttons and rail stops on phones.
- **Do** treat "Trao" and the logo as replaceable, keeping them out of any rule that would break on a rename.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put product, price or form content on blurred glass.
- **Don't** use red for urgency, favourites, required markers or decoration, or as a background for a whole flash-sale section.
- **Don't** set white text on a solid sky fill.
- **Don't** use `--blue-*` as a brand colour, or `--brand-*` to mean information.
- **Don't** use orange as a brand colour, or anything that makes the page read as Shopee, Lazada or Tiki. Marigold stays at hue 85 or above so it reads yellow, never orange.
- **Don't** fill a band, hero or section with royal blue, or bring back blue-to-blue gradients.
- **Don't** bring glass, pills or the light field into Vendor or Admin, and don't bring their dark ink chrome into the storefront.
- **Don't** hard-code a dashboard figure that can be derived from the mock data it summarises.
